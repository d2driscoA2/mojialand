Put it on your Home Screen

iPhone

  1. 1Open the game in Safari, then tap Share.
  2. 2Tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. 3Keep Open as Web App on, then tap Add.
  4. 4Tap the new icon. Full screen, and your pass comes along.

On newer iPhones, Share may sit under the ••• button.

iPad

On iPad, tap Share at the top of Safari, then View More, then Add to Home Screen.
