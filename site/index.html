Help

Questions, fixes, and a real person

How does free play work?
Each device gets 10 free minutes a day. The timer lives on the device only. When time runs out, your child sees a pause picture and a Grown-up button. Prices never appear on kid screens.
How do I use my pass on another device?
Open Mojialand on the new device, hold the gear button for 3 seconds, and tap Restore. Enter the code from your email (the code starts with MOJI). The restore link in your receipt email works too.
How do I add Mojialand to my home screen?
On iPhone or iPad:
  1. Open Mojialand in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
On Android, open the Chrome menu and tap Add to Home screen.
How do refunds work?
Ask within 7 days of any purchase and we refund the full amount. Send a message with the form here, and include the email you used at checkout.
No sound on iPhone?
Flip the ring or silent switch on the side of the phone, turn the volume up, then tap the screen once. In Grown-ups, tap Sound check to test.
Where are the grown-up settings?
Hold the gear button on the home screen for 3 seconds. Grown-ups holds Voice, Sounds, How-to-play cards, and the Welcome screen replay.

Privacy notice

Effective September 23, 2026

The short version: Mojialand collects nothing from your child. Grown-ups share an email address and payment details only when buying a pass, and Stripe handles the card.

Who runs Mojialand

What we collect from children

Nothing. Kid screens have no text fields, no microphone, no camera, and no location. The game sends no requests to any outside company. Mojialand uses no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking cookies.

What stays on the device

The browser on your device saves game settings (sound, voice, how-to cards), the free play timer, and your pass token. This data stays in the browser's local storage and never reaches us. Clearing your browser data removes the data.

What we collect from grown-ups

  • When you buy a pass: your email address and payment details, entered on Stripe's secure form.
  • From Stripe we keep: your email, a restore code, the pass type, the expiry date, the Stripe session ID, and how many devices use the code.
  • When you send a message: your email, the topic, and your message.

We use this data to deliver your pass, restore the pass on your devices, send receipts, answer your questions, and process refunds. We never sell or rent data, and we never use data for advertising.

Who processes data for us

  • Stripe: payments and receipts. Stripe's privacy policy applies to card details.
  • Netlify: website hosting and the contact form.
  • Our email provider: restore code emails.

How long we keep data

  • 48-hour pass records: deleted 30 days after the pass expires.
  • Forever pass records: kept while the pass exists.
  • Support messages: deleted 12 months after the conversation ends.
  • Stripe keeps payment records as Stripe's own legal duties require.

Your choices

Ask us to see or delete your data any time. Use the contact form and pick Privacy request, or email Contact email to confirm. We reply within 30 days. Deleting a Forever pass record ends the pass.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

Mojialand is made for children under 13 and follows the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act. We collect no personal information from children. Every purchase, restore, and outside link sits behind a grown-up check.
