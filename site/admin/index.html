Printable cards

Business cards, 10 to a letter sheet, 3 passes on each card. Every code is its own family pass with a QR code and the link. Use the kind, reason and use-by days above. The codes show once, here and on the sheet.

Support inbox

Messages from the in-app contact form. Reply from the Mojialand inbox, then mark done.

Phone alerts

Your phone buzzes for each new player and each gift code used. Alerts never name a family, an email, a device, or a place.

Settings

The game reads these on the next open. Numbers are minutes or days. Reset time is 24-hour, like 04:00.

Services

Every outside service Mojialand runs on. Passwords live in Apple Passwords. Secrets live in Netlify.
